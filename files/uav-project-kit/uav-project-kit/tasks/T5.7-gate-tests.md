## Background

This gate has two jobs: confirm each realism component behaves correctly, and
confirm the whole system is **bit-reproducible from a seed**. The second is not
optional. Phase 9's entire credibility rests on it, and it is far cheaper to
enforce now than to discover in month eight that campaign results cannot be
reproduced.

## Notes

If the reproducibility test fails, the cause is almost always a stray
`np.random.*` call, a `set` or `dict` iteration order affecting float summation,
or a filter that seeds itself from the clock. Grep for `np.random.` across `src/`
first — it takes ten seconds and finds it most of the time.
