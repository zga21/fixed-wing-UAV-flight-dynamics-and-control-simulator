# Phase 4 Baseline Controller

The Phase 4 baseline is a cascaded PID autopilot with a 200 Hz body-rate loop,
50 Hz attitude loop, and 10 Hz outer loop. The inner loops use the Phase 3
linearisation as the design reference: roll, pitch, and yaw are treated as
decoupled near symmetric trim, while the attitude loop applies coordinated-turn
yaw-rate feedforward to avoid exciting sideslip during heading changes.

The outer loop is TECS-lite: throttle acts on total energy and pitch acts on the
energy balance between altitude and airspeed. The gains are intentionally exposed
as one flat vector with names and bounds, so Phase 7 can optimise this baseline
with the same machinery used for later nonlinear controllers. The committed
baseline record in `docs/baseline_record.json` is regenerated by
`python scripts/record_baseline.py`.
