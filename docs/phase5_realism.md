# Phase 5 Realism Models

Phase 5 leaves the frozen aircraft and Phase 4 controller unchanged and adds
realism around them. Each control surface and the throttle use a first-order lag,
then a rate limit, then a mechanical position limit. The actuators advance once
per 500 Hz simulation step and begin each scenario at the trimmed command, which
represents an aircraft already established in flight rather than servos starting
from an artificial zero-deflection transient.

The sensor suite has independent seeded streams, sensor-specific update rates,
zero-order hold, white noise, and random-walk bias. A 15-error-state navigation
EKF propagates position, velocity, quaternion attitude, and IMU biases at the
200 Hz IMU cadence. GPS, barometer, pitot, and magnetometer updates use Joseph
covariance form. Accelerometer attitude updates reject dynamic specific force,
so turbulence and manoeuvre acceleration are not mistaken for gravity.

Steady wind uses a meteorological direction convention and optional power-law
shear. Dryden turbulence is generated by discretised shaping filters: a
first-order longitudinal filter and second-order lateral/vertical filters with
the standard additional zero. The configured light, moderate, and severe levels
use vertical standard deviations of 1, 2, and 4 m/s. Wind is held through each
RK4 step, transformed into body axes by the air-data boundary, and never injected
directly into rigid-body ground velocity.

The estimator deliberately remains a compact research-support model rather than
a flight-certification navigation stack. It does not estimate wind or separate
barometer, pitot, and magnetometer biases as states; their random walks are
represented through conservative measurement covariance. Both Phase 4 and the
later Phase 6 controller use this same estimate in realistic comparisons.
